DUSTLINE — a tactical 3v3 bomb-defusal FPS on three.js. You and two bot teammates hold an abandoned solar plant in a dust basin against three enemy bots: two sites (Solar Array and Pump House), five fictional weapons plus two grenades, a round economy with a buy phase, and bots that hold angles, rotate between sites, and plant or defuse the spike. First to 5 rounds wins.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.mjs
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser with WebGL 2. Click to lock the mouse. WASD to move, click to fire, R to reload, B for the buy menu, E to plant or defuse, G to throw a grenade.
